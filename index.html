Our Origin

From the Kóló Traditional Saving to KoloRise

The cultural wisdom of saving, reimagined for the digital generation.

The Traditional Kóló

In Nigerian culture, particularly within Yoruba communities, kóló traditionally refers to a local handmade money box or piggy bank. It represents more than just a container; it represents discipline, patience, consistency, and the willingness to sacrifice today for a better tomorrow.

The Digital Evolution

Growing up and seeing how familiar this concept is within our culture inspired a vital question: What if the philosophy behind the traditional kóló could be brought into the modern digital world? That question became the beginning of KoloRise.

The Meaning of Rise

After saving comes rising—rising financially, in confidence, and toward meaningful goals. Combining Kóló (cultural foundation) with Rise (progress and destination) created KoloRise: the journey from saving to growth.

Meet The Founder

David Ayodele

Founder & CEO, KoloRise

KoloRise started as an idea built around a simple observation: many people understand the importance of saving, but developing and maintaining a consistent saving habit can be difficult.

As a young Nigerian, I wanted to explore how technology could make saving more structured, accessible, and relevant to a generation growing up in a digital world.

"KoloRise started with a simple question: how can we bring the familiar habit of saving into the digital world? The journey begins with saving, and the goal is to grow."
